<p>WiTV is a live TV player built for Android TV and TV boxes. Everything works with a remote control.</p>

<p>WiTV does not ship any channels or content. You add your own legitimate M3U playlists.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Manage several M3U / M3U8 playlists and switch between them</li>
<li>Merges multiple stream URLs per channel and fails over automatically</li>
<li>EPG from the playlist's XMLTV URL or one you set yourself</li>
<li>Web admin on your LAN: add playlists and favorites from a phone or PC browser</li>
<li>UDP / RTP multicast, with optional udpxy unicast proxy</li>
<li>Built-in FFmpeg software decoding for formats the box can't decode</li>
<li>Can replace the home screen and start straight into live TV</li>
<li>Chinese and English UI, two themes</li>
</ul>
